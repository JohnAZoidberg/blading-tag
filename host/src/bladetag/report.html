What the strokes say

Correlations are Spearman's rank r between strokes. "Within a session" removes each session's median first, so it shows what changes from one stroke to the next, not what differs between days, feet or wind. Strokes under 5 km/h are left out.

Along the way

Map

Each dot is a stroke, coloured by the measure picked above, set off to the right of the direction of travel like lanes on a road, so going out and coming back sit side by side.

Same place, compared

Strokes from two sessions within 15 m of each other. On the same road the surface and slope are the same, so what's left is wind, tiredness, the foot and how you skated.

At one common speed

Coasting

While coasting nothing pushes, so the speed lost per second is rolling resistance plus air drag. Coast at the same spot in both directions at the same speed and the difference is the wind.
